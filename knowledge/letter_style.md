Used by `career-cover-letter-writer` and `qa-fact-checker`.
Derived from Tim's own letters (`knowledge/letter_examples.md`). Updated after every feedback round and every outcome.

## Tailoring to the organisation (Tim's top priority, 2026-11-24)
Every letter must be written for THIS organisation. Test: if you could swap the organisation's name and the letter still works, it is not tailored enough.
- **Research first** (their own website, the vacancy page, "over ons"/mission pages): mission, motto or values in their own words, what the team/department does, who they serve, their tone (je/u), and what the role must make possible for them.
- **At least four organisation-specific elements in the letter:**
  1. The opening hook mirrors their work: a scene from Tim's experience that shows the same thing the role needs (Visma: "de juiste mensen op het juiste moment op de juiste plek" → "renners, coaches en stafleden altijd op de juiste plek").
  2. One sentence that names their specific situation or people (riders and staff, residents, researchers, children with a disability), not a generic "jullie organisatie".
  3. Their motto, mission or values, quoted correctly and only if found on their own site (Visma: "beyond victory"), linked to something true about Tim.
  4. Tim's personal affinity with their world, if it is in `profile.md` (cycling/Tour de France for a cycling team, football/HFC for a football organisation, movement science for research, vulnerable youth for youth organisations).
- What Tim wants to learn and from whom must also be specific to them ("van collega's die precies weten hoe een koers achter de schermen werkt").

## Structure (blueprint from four letters Tim approved, Nov 2026)
1. **Opening (± 80 words):** concrete personal hook from Tim's experience (Buenos Aires/La Matanza worked in every letter), linked to what the organisation needs; end the paragraph with the role: "Daarom solliciteer ik als/naar …" — the goal must be clear in paragraph 1.
2. **Introduction (± 60 words):** name, age, bachelor (HvA) completed, gap year before the pre-master; why the timing/hours fit; languages if the role needs them.
3. **Main experience (± 80 words):** the experience that best matches the requirements (usually the Sint Jacob afstudeerstage: "Mijn afstudeerstage werd met een 10 beoordeeld"), then one short skill sentence (Excel, Word, wiskunde B as problem-solving; AI when the role involves planning, searching or admin).
4. **Honest gap + learning (± 90 words):** first Tim's own related experience (e.g. self-planned travel for a travel role), then "Eerlijk is eerlijk: …" for what he lacks, then what he wants to learn there, responsibility and network.
5. **Organisation-specific close (± 60 words):** personal affinity with their world, their motto/mission, a short personality line (energetic, ondernemend, leergierig) and an invitation for a conversation.
- Length: 380–400 words is normal for Tim; hard maximum 400. When Tim adds something, cut the least relevant detail (side events, HFC when not relevant) and tell him what was cut.

## Tone
- Language of the vacancy (Dutch or English). Dutch: "je/jullie" for informal organisations, "u" for formal ones (follow the vacancy).
- Personal but businesslike: personal details are welcome, phrased formally ("in mijn familie heeft die altijd een grote plek", not "is dat altijd een groot ding"; no "vanaf de bank").
- Coordination, not teaching: Tim coordinated La Matanza; he did not give the lessons himself.

## Before calling something a gap
Check `profile.md` for related personal experience (travel, hobbies, family, side jobs) before writing that Tim lacks something. If nothing is there, ask Tim in the notes ("Vragen voor Tim"): e.g. "Volg je wielrennen?" turned into a strong paragraph for Visma.

## Rules
- Only facts from `knowledge/profile.md`. Never invent achievements, numbers or experience.
- Age is always taken from `profile.md`.
- No exaggerated claims like "de perfecte fit" without supporting evidence (keep it confident but grounded).
- Skills: Tim wants one short skill sentence in his letters, also when the vacancy does not ask for it ("Daarnaast werk ik goed met Excel en Word en ben ik sterk in wiskunde B."). Never as self-study effort ("I worked through the whole maths textbook"): that does not sound convincing (Tim, 2026-11-18; confirmed by his choices for Mulier and Happy2Move).
- Spell-check: "hart" (not "hard"), "geld ophalen" (not "geld ophouden"), "op zoek" (two words), "doordat/door middel van".
