# Letter style guide — Tim Aarts

Used by `career-cover-letter-writer` and `qa-fact-checker`.
Derived from Tim's own letters (`knowledge/letter_examples.md`). Updated after every feedback round and every outcome.

## Tone & structure (what works in Tim's letters)
- Dutch, personal, energetic; "je/jullie" for informal organisations, "u" for formal ones (follow the vacancy's tone).
- Open with a concrete, personal hook linked to the organisation (not a generic sentence).
- Short self-introduction: name, age, current situation.
- 1–2 concrete experiences with specific details (La Matanza, thesis with grade 10, T.A Vitality) — show, don't tell.
- Explicit link between experience and the organisation's mission.
- Close with a brief personality line (energetic, social, eager to learn, humour) and an invitation for a conversation.
- Length: 250–400 words.

## Rules
- Only facts from `knowledge/profile.md`. Never invent achievements, numbers or experience.
- Age is always taken from `profile.md`.
- No exaggerated claims like "de perfecte fit" without supporting evidence (keep it confident but grounded).
- Skills: Tim wants one short skill sentence in his letters, also when the vacancy does not ask for it ("Daarnaast werk ik goed met Excel en Word en ben ik sterk in wiskunde B."). Never as self-study effort ("I worked through the whole maths textbook"): that does not sound convincing (Tim, 2026-11-18; confirmed by his choices for Mulier and Happy2Move).
- Spell-check: "hart" (not "hard"), "geld ophalen" (not "geld ophouden"), "op zoek" (two words), "doordat/door middel van".
