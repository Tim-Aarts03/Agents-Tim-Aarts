# Event preferences — Tim Aarts

Used by `career-event-scout`. Scores are computed by `tools/events.py`, never by the LLM.

## Goal (Tim, 2026-11-20)
Go to **one networking event per month** that improves his future: meet people who can help him toward a
traineeship or job with big impact, a strong network and many learning situations (same goals as
`knowledge/job_preferences.md`).

## What Tim told us (2026-11-20)
- **No price limit.** Show every event with its price; Tim's ratings will teach what is worth it. Never buy
  tickets or register for Tim.
- **Available any day and any time** (weekdays, evenings, weekends).
- **Doesn't know exactly what he likes yet.** The scout casts a wide net; Tim rates every event 1–5 and
  the learned bonus (`tools/events.py`) does the rest. Fixed weights are deliberately small.

## Starting assumptions (from the job preferences; to be confirmed by ratings)
- Topics close to his goals: sport for vulnerable groups, sport policy, sport & society / social impact,
  sports business and brands (Upfront, adidas, UEFA Foundation are dream employers), movement science,
  careers & traineeships for starters.
- Network value comes from **who is there**: people from dream employers or policy organisations,
  recruiters, decision makers; small groups make talking easier.
- Organisations excluded for jobs (FIFA, Red Bull, Atleetfabriek in `knowledge/job_filters.json`) are also
  excluded as organisers.
